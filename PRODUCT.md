# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** International investors evaluating real estate and hospitality opportunities in Serbia — typically institutional or private-equity-minded buyers who need investor-grade analysis, regulatory clarity, and access to off-market deals before committing capital. They arrive at the site to judge whether the firm is credible enough to engage.
- **Secondary:** Domestic stakeholders — landowners, developers, financial institutions, and authorities — who interact with the firm's mandates. The Serbian language version serves them.

## Product Purpose

Public marketing website for the consulting firm MaLa Luminova Partners. Confirmed structure:

- **Main page:** hero section, a section presenting the firm's 4 services, an About Us section, and a contact form section.
- **Service detail pages:** one dedicated page per service, carrying the full detail of that service.

Success means the site reads at the credibility level of a serious bank and converts qualified visitors into inquiries through the contact form.

## Positioning

Direct access to senior experts — board-level governance experience (Audit Committee President / Board Member of a regulated financial institution), CFO/Group-CFO-level financial leadership, and private equity discipline — combined with privileged access to off-market Serbian real estate opportunities and proprietary deal flow. Big 4 assurance foundations and IFRS-grade documentation aligned with international standards, in a market where that combination is scarce. Tagline: **"Financial Insight. Strategic Structure. Capital Execution."**

## Operating Context

Serbian real estate and hospitality sectors; cross-border transactions for foreign investors. Work spans the full real estate investment cycle: sourcing high-value locations, feasibility and commercial due diligence, transaction structuring within Serbian regulatory and planning frameworks, and coordination among landowners, developers, authorities, and financial institutions. Experience includes distressed-debt portfolio acquisitions from the Deposit Insurance Agency (AOD), giving insight into Serbia's insolvency, enforcement, and asset recovery frameworks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **The 4 services** (from Advisor_Profile.pdf, "What We Do"):
  1. **Location sourcing & market access** — identification of high-potential sites and assets, including off-market opportunities and proprietary deal flow.
  2. **Commercial & financial assessment** — investor-grade feasibility analysis, financial modeling, and commercial due diligence.
  3. **Transaction structuring** — support in negotiations, deal structuring, and alignment with regulatory and planning frameworks.
  4. **Stakeholder coordination** — engagement with landowners, developers, authorities, and financial institutions.
- **Languages:** English + Serbian, bilingual from the start.
- **Conversion path:** the contact form is the public contact method.
- **Client references must NOT be published.** The reference list in Advisor_Profile.pdf (ALTA banka, Samsung Electronics Adriatic, Delhaize Serbia, Delta Holding, Abris PE, PwC, and others) is confirmed off-limits for public surfaces.
- **Open decisions (do not invent):**
  - Whether team members (Lela Arsic, Jelena Pantovic) are named publicly in About Us — confirm before naming individuals. Their personal emails/phones from the PDF should be assumed private unless the user says otherwise.
  - Firm domain, public email address, and where the contact form submits.
  - Hosting and tech stack.

## Brand Commitments

- **Full name:** MaLa Luminova Partners. **Abbreviation:** ML (as in the monogram).
- **Logo:** `MaLa-Luminova-Partners-2B-lockup.png` — navy serif "ML" monogram, gold vertical rule, spaced sans-serif "LUMINOVA PARTNERS" wordmark on cream.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Advisor_Profile.pdf` — the authoritative source for all service descriptions, core strengths (governance & regulatory insight, CFO-level financial leadership, PE/M&A/distressed-asset experience, analytical rigor & multinational standards), and positioning language. Extracted text: usable copy basis for the site.
- `MaLa-Luminova-Partners-2B-lockup.png` — the only brand asset.
- **Absent — must not be fabricated:** testimonials, case studies with numbers, deal-volume metrics, client logos (references exist but are barred from publication), team photos, office address, firm domain/email.

## Product Principles

1. **Credibility over flash.** The visitor is deciding whether to trust the firm with capital; banking-grade restraint is the strategy, not a limitation.
2. **Every claim traces to the profile.** Copy derives from Advisor_Profile.pdf; never invent clients, numbers, testimonials, or track-record specifics.
3. **Two readers, full parity.** The English and Serbian versions carry the same substance — neither is a stub of the other.
4. **Discretion is part of the brand.** No client names, no personal contact details; the contact form is the door.
5. **Each service page stands alone.** A service detail page must be complete and credible when shared as a direct link, not just as a continuation of the main page.
